# Security policy

Please report security problems privately to **mail@xuanhieu.org** with the subject "Security: 1xQuickConvertFormat". Include the app version (Settings › About), your macOS version and steps to reproduce. Do not open a public issue for security reports.

You will get a reply within 7 days. Fixes ship as a new signed and notarized release, and the release notes credit the reporter unless you prefer otherwise.

In scope: the macOS app, its license activation, and the license and purchase service at `1x.xuanhieu.org`. Out of scope: FFmpeg and LibreOffice themselves (report those upstream), denial-of-service and social-engineering tests.

---

Báo lỗ hổng bảo mật qua email **mail@xuanhieu.org** (tiêu đề "Security: 1xQuickConvertFormat"), kèm phiên bản app, phiên bản macOS và các bước tái hiện. Vui lòng không tạo issue công khai. Bạn sẽ nhận phản hồi trong vòng 7 ngày.
